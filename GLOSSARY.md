# Cadence

An online course platform where instructors sell courses and Purchasers buy them, either for themselves or for a team.

## Language

### People

**Purchaser**:
The person who paid for a Purchase. On a Team Purchase, the Purchaser is often not a Student of the course.
_Avoid_: Buyer, customer, learner

**Student**:
A person with an Enrollment in a course.
_Avoid_: Learner

### Buying and access

**Purchase**:
A Purchaser's payment for a course. It gives either the Purchaser's own Enrollment or, on a Team Purchase, a set of Seats.
_Avoid_: Order, transaction

**Enrollment**:
A person's access to a course.
_Avoid_: Access, membership

**Team Purchase**:
A Purchase of several Seats in a course for other people to use.
_Avoid_: Team licence, bulk purchase

**Seat**:
One person's place in a Team Purchase, handed out through a Coupon.
_Avoid_: Licence

**Coupon**:
The code that hands out one Seat of a Team Purchase.
_Avoid_: Invite, seat code

**Redeem**:
To use a Coupon to take its Seat, which gives the redeemer an Enrollment.
_Avoid_: Claim

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A Purchaser's ask to get their money back on a Purchase. It is Pending until it is Approved or Declined, and it is not a Refund until it is Approved.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, chargeback, reversal; "open", "requested", "raised" (as states); "refund" (for an ask that has not been Approved)

**Refund**:
Money returned to the Purchaser for a Purchase after their Refund Request is Approved. A Refund takes back whatever the Purchase gave.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, chargeback, reversal

**Refund Amount**:
The money a Refund returns, which is always exactly what the Purchaser paid for the Purchase.
_Avoid_: Refundable amount, list price

**Refund Eligibility**:
Whether a Purchaser may make a Refund Request on a Purchase right now.
_Avoid_: Cancellation offer

**Refund Window**:
The period of calendar days after a Purchase during which a Refund Request can be made.
_Avoid_: Cancel window, cancellation period
